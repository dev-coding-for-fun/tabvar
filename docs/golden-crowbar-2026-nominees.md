# 2026 Golden Crowbar nominees

The Golden Crowbar celebrates the people who put in the work to create and care for our climbing. This year's nominees have developed new crags, put up sport and multipitch routes, established canyon descents and replaced aging hardware.

## Ethan Woodside

Ethan put up two three-pitch sport routes on the Armadillo Buttress in Grotto Canyon: [A Fine Place for a Friend](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/grotto-canyon#route-4079) (5.10b) and [Armadillo Adventure](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/grotto-canyon#route-4078) (5.10a).

## Rhys Beaudry & Joel Freund

Rhys and Joel built [Cloud Break at Bataan](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/bataan#sector-1248) from the ground up, developing five walls and nearly 30 published routes in 2025. Their lines range from 5.10 to hard 5.13, with a lot of climbing in between.

## Brendan Clark

Brendan developed and renewed 11 pitches at [Antiques Crag on Mount Baldy](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/mt-baldy#sector-1254), established the [Side Door](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/door-jamb-mountain#route-3655) ascent line on Door Jamb's east ridge, and worked on descents in [Arch Canyon](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/mount-mcgillivray#sector-1256), [Nihahi Canyon](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/nihahi-canyon) and [Prairie Dog Canyon](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/prairie-dog-canyon).

## Jeff Moore

Jeff put up a collection of single-pitch lines at [Buffalo Wall](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/buffalo-crag#sector-1210), including sport routes and the mixed climb Guide Cracker. He also developed [Terristo](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/black-feather-canyon#route-3212) and [Downway](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/black-feather-canyon#route-4221) at Black Feather Canyon.

## Steve Fedyna

Steve put up [It's Not Over Until You Bleed](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/old-goat-crag#route-4089), a 5.12a sport pitch at Old Goat Crag.

## Evan Hau

At the Coliseum's [Saddle Up Wall](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/echo-canyon#sector-799), Evan finished bolting [Queen Bee Direct](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/echo-canyon#route-4126), a line mostly bolted by Matt Pieterson, and bolted another hard project to its right. That second line was unnamed and awaiting a redpoint at the time.

## Taylor Hertzsprung

Taylor put up three single-pitch sport climbs at [the Back Door in Grotto Canyon](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/grotto-canyon#sector-1259): Underwear?, Two Pump Chump and In the Stripped Club. Taylor also established [First Litter](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/cat-creek-crag#route-4188), a 5.9 at Cat Creek Crag.

## Daniel Padeanu

Daniel helped build up [Birdwatcher's Crag](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/birdwatchers-crag), putting up 17 single-pitch climbs across the Sandcastle, Old Growth and the Farside.

## Sara Lilley

Sara replaced 26 aging bolts on [Premature](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/ha-ling-peak#route-3959) on Ha Ling, putting in the work to maintain an existing multipitch route.

## Chris Perry

Chris put up [Forever Yonge](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/tunnel-mountain#route-3681) on Tunnel Mountain, including its No Country for Old Men and OCD pitch variations. He also developed [Running On Empty](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/tunnel-mountain#route-4253).

## Marcus Norman

Marcus put up four sport routes at [Middle Ace](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/acephale#sector-1262) and expanded [Punter Palace](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/tunnel-mountain#sector-1261) on Tunnel Mountain. His Punter Palace work includes Fury Road, its extension, Guided by Angels and Burger o'Clock.

## Madison Marchuck

Madison bolted the [Groove Project](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/acephale#route-4125) at upper Acéphale, retrofitted nearby [Angst](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/acephale#route-795), and developed the [Light Roast project](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/moose-mountain#route-2035) at Moose Mountain's Black Flag sector.

## Mason Tessier

Mason developed seven lines across [the Coliseum](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/echo-canyon#sector-799), [Atlantis](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/echo-canyon#route-4129) and [the Outhouse area at Lake Louise](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/lake-louise-back-of-the-lake#sector-998). His work includes hard sport climbing at the Coliseum and Atlantis, and two Lake Louise lines that were open projects in 2025.

## Grant Parkin

Grant put up rock routes at [the Shady Wall on Mount Baldy](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/mt-baldy#sector-1255) and on Door Jamb, where his work includes [Duality Direct](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/door-jamb-mountain#route-4135) and [Peanut Butter](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/door-jamb-mountain#route-4132). He also replaced anchors on ice routes.

## Brian Garrison

Brian developed ten routes across [Middle Ace](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/acephale#sector-1262), [Punter Palace](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/tunnel-mountain#sector-1261) and [Prairie Creek](https://app.tabvar.org/topos/prairie-creek). His work includes Sputnik Sweetheart and new lines at Petra, the Korova Milk Bar and the Libido Cafe.
